This is a positive-duration prescribed-interval value, without an owner or a second clock. A native TT0 force constructor and resident admission are separate increments.

The caller supplies the complete immutable reference and prepared LAW44 material. Material curve backing is borrowed and must outlive every retained history and evaluation. Histories retain exact reference/source identities, all six native material strain channels, forward curve cursors, filtered strain rate, actual density and storage volume, internal energy density, plastic work, and bulk pressure. Every public write is staged; late point, geometry, phase, identity, or nonfinite failure leaves the supplied output unchanged.

Current coordinates and velocities retain all eight original source slots. The admitted collapsed source pattern is `[A,B,C,D,E,E,F,F]`; repeated NIDs require bit-identical current coordinates and velocities. Native geometry orientation may permute slots, and forces are restored to original slots after native frame rotation. Neither histories nor force terms are coalesced by NID.

Ordinary IDEG0 subtracts point mean stress and adds the reduced center pressure afterward.

No unique-node division or solid rotational stiffness is implied.
