# Original-source wall refinement comparison

`robo_dyna_source_part_wall_compare H_DIR H2_DIR H4_DIR NEW_JSON` reads the three strict accepted wall bundles and writes a create-only report. It requires the frozen 32768 H physical horizon and all 257 common frames. Accepted prefixes remain available to replay but cannot pass full refinement. It compares the complete physical configuration, source mappings and native M/J tables, wall certificates and actual placed-wall bytes; only refinement and output ownership/cadence metadata are excluded.

The four kinematic channels reuse `SourcePartComparison.h`. Additional channels are synchronized kinetic plus native total work plus wall potential /0.1 J, wall potential /K0, carried wall impulse /(M V), and reaction /(2 K0 /design depth). K0 is the archived measured common startup energy; M is the long-double sum of all 117 native node masses rounded once for the comparison scale. Every channel retains the frozen .10/.05/.8 contraction gates with 1e-6 floor. Every run must show at least 10 micrometres of actual chord change and finite strain/curvature inside the frozen envelopes.

Onset and rebound brackets are reconstructed from every accepted interval, including unsaved endpoints. First onset is bracketed by its adjacent accepted times; the actual initial-gap certificate width divided by the declared speed supplies its timing uncertainty. Bracket agreement uses one combined coarser-step expansion plus both certificate uncertainties. Rebound requires a terminal continuous run of 117 strictly separated nodes, zero force/potential within certificates and a strictly negative conservative upper bound on raw carried COM momentum. The reconstruction uses initial native momentum minus cumulative wall kick impulse plus the recorded residual, including impulse error, the declared momentum allowance and arithmetic rounding. Saved raw native mass-weighted velocities cross-check that reconstruction. A run of N separated endpoint samples spans (N-1) intervals; qualifying duration must reach 128 base-step durations. A later touch or uncertain/nonnegative COM resets the terminal run. Its event bracket records separation onset, not the later confirmation time.

Exit 0 and `qualified_completed_rebound` require every gate plus completed terminal rebound. Exit 2 and `qualified_partial_impact` retain otherwise-qualified deforming impact that has not separated by the fixed horizon. Exit 1 indicates invalid input or failed comparison/deformation/event gates. A partial result does not authorize changing the physics or extending the horizon without a separately frozen same-physics plan. No material solver, contact solver, state owner or native oracle runs in this comparator.

`source-part-wall-output-comparison-tests-2`, covering physical identity,
normalization/convergence, contact-event persistence and rejected observations.
Actual h/h2/h4 trajectory comparison is a separate required numerical gate.
