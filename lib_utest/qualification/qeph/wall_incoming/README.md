# CW2 incoming-entry prefix: prospective execution contract

This qualification composes the existing sole nodal owner, resident QEPH
internal-force/history cache, and stateless finite-wall contributor. It tests
entry into contact through a short, independently checked native recurrence.
It is source-complete preparation for three actual CUDA functions; no numerical
execution or full impact-response acceptance is claimed by this contract.

## Screen binding and frozen experiment

Root must authenticate the reviewed selector executable/provenance and the
complete, passed final selection archive before launching this test. Set
`TL_CW2_SELECTED_H` to exactly `0x1p-24` or `0x1p-25`, matching its selected step,
and `TL_CW2_SCREEN_SHA256` to the lowercase SHA256 of that archive's final
`index.json`. The final index binds `selection.json`, all four boost comparison
files and their provenance. The test records this exact index SHA; checking its
format does not authenticate the archive. There is no default step, skipped GPU
test, decision-file parser in CUDA code, or selection inferred from a job's
aggregate pass flag. Missing or malformed input rejects before owner allocation.

Both fixtures consume the immutable `WallRecurrenceModel` used by the screen:
one/two 20 mm Q4 cells, four/six free nodes, actual native translation mass and
total isotropic J, and separate physical/added rotary partitions. The reference
coordinates are the native reference at `X=-InitialGap`, not the disposable
touching matrix-probe state at X=0. Material is the same declared LAW1 experiment
`E=200e9 Pa`, `nu=.3`, `rho=7890 kg/m3`, `t=.001648 m`. No source MAT024 or
full-vehicle material equivalence is implied.

The incoming speed is exactly 8 m/s, target depth .000375 m, initial gap
target/4, and depth cap .0005 m. The exact binary64 stiffness-per-area is copied
from `WallRecurrenceModel::law()`, including its directed upward penalty-chain
construction; it is never replaced by a rounded decimal estimate. Parent area
and share certificates are checked against that model after preparation.
The finite two-triangle `Square()` wall, Y/Z motion box ±.05 m, 1e-6 m clearance,
and per-parent absolute errors `5e-7 N` / `1.2500000000000005e-12 J` remain fixed.
The fixture has no support constraints, rotational input or external load.

## Interval order and independent gates

Each interval first computes contact from the independently accepted native
base, then calls the existing native `Propose`. Device assembly subtracts the
accepted shell cache and adds contact at its accepted base. The actual owner
performs the first h/2 kick, subsequent h kicks, and an h drift. Candidate shell
and wall checks alternate order. Complete native force/history and owner parity,
independent assembly/work/impulse ledgers, rigid-response checks and entry-event
checks all finish before the existing single `CommitQephTrial` call. Afterwards
only fixed-size host event/contact/native values are assigned. No new force,
allocation, validation or device read occurs after owner commit in publication.

The crossing drift must have an entirely separated base, zero base force and
potential, positive candidate gap/potential at every node, and unchanged incoming
velocity. Its endpoint epoch must lie in the frozen scalar entry window ±1.
All masks are empty or full using the owning `X>=wall_x` convention. The following
kick is the first to consume positive contact; its maximum normal velocity is
strictly below 8 m/s. The scalar schedule is used for the prospective event
window, never to fit a measured event or replace the native force trajectory.

The expected broadside motion is rigid. Regular and HG rates, numeric history,
source work and internal cache stay within the retained dimensional roundoff
budgets. CW0's required nonzero omitted-shell signal does not apply to this
fixture. Zero numerical history checks retain the actual active flag because
full native parity independently checks that flag. Source EINT[2] and EVIS are
separately checked against a zero-work bound using K0, in addition to all existing
native field and aggregate-work comparisons.

The independent ledgers use long-double sums of the actual stored operands:
`256*epsilon64*sum(abs(terms)) + 1e-12*physical_scale`, plus the existing contact
certificate/work uncertainty where applicable. The positive scales are fixed
before the first interval: `K0=sum(.5*m_i*8^2)`, `P0=sum(m_i*8)`, `L0=Side*P0`.
Total J is used directly for rotational kinetic/work; physical/added partitions
are only reported separately. There is no mass normalization or recombination.
Native field agreement stays at its existing 2e-12 dimensional coefficient.
Zero regular rates use `2e-12*8/Side` (first five) or `2e-12*8/Side^2` (last three).
HG indices 2/3 use `2e-12*8/Side`; the other four use `2e-12*8`. The retained
owner `VelocityBudget()` and `SpinBudget()` remain unchanged.

Two wrong-order controls must exceed 32 budget units: omitting the accepted-base
contact after entry, and prematurely consuming candidate contact at crossing.
Each velocity budget includes the retained owner velocity budget, 256 epsilon
times absolute velocity terms, and `kick_dt*inverse_mass` times the actual
force-certificate errors plus the retained dimensional shell-force uncertainty
`2e-12*(E*t*length+abs(force))` from base and candidate caches. Candidate
uncertainty is included for the premature control. Signals and budgets are saved
separately from their ratios. These controls are not a replacement trajectory.

## Three functions and bounded work

1. One-cell incoming entry at selected h and h/2, including malformed typed
   screen bindings before allocation and immutable fixture initialization.
2. Shared two-cell entry at selected h and h/2, with native cache/scatter,
   contact and independent kinetic/momentum/work checks before every receipt.
3. One shared two-cell pre-entry state, seven semantic rejection variants, exact
   inspect/discard retry after each, then accepted crossing and first contact
   kick. Faults are late shell geometry after wall success, late contact cap after
   shell success, foreign owner, stale contact attempt, foreign shell config,
   failed receipt/unpublished event, and changed wall binding. They preserve all
   accepted owner/history/K0/contact/event/native bytes and do not rerun a native
   proposal on each retry.

The two complete prefixes each end at exactly `224*2^-24 s`, before peak contact:

| Selected step | Intervals at h / h2, per fixture | Native cell intervals, both complete fixtures | Failure fixture native cell intervals | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `2^-24 s` | 224 / 448 | 2016 | 396 | 2412 |
| `2^-25 s` | 448 / 896 | 4032 | 790 | 4822 |

Failure counts follow the frozen nominal entry epoch (197 or 394), with one
native proposal per accepted interval and one reused crossing proposal. Repeated
parity comparisons do not create extra native force calls. Event storage is a
fixed array of 896 intervals. Device allocations remain the existing owner,
QEPH batch and wall allocations, checked unchanged after each complete-prefix
interval and around all failures. Actual byte counts remain unmeasured until
root's bounded CUDA execution; this source contract does not guess an ABI.

Reports distinguish the configured prefix horizon from actual accepted time in
the shorter failure fixture. They record the final selection-index SHA, step,
accepted/native counts, event epochs/gaps, independent scales, ledger maxima,
control signal/budget/ratios and measured allocations. They make no claim about
peak depth/force, release/rebound, full h/h2/h4 convergence or renderable output.

--prec-sqrt=true --ftz=false -Xcompiler=-fno-fast-math,-ffp-contract=off`.

top-level registration, source map or runtime is edited by this source slice.

and default ledger scales. `SameNumerics` is moved verbatim from the old private
failure helper into `WallCoupledResultChecks.h` for shared exact retry checks.
Root reruns the four CW0 functions, affected coupled-ledger/response and uniform
startup regressions, and the new three functions after source-map freeze and
the actual selected-step decision are authenticated.
