# Complete physical publication

The explicit `ShellBatchPublication::InitializePhysical` profile attaches the
actual mapped QEPH/T3/QBAT and TYPE25 batches, the TYPE13 resident and the shared
three-family solid resident. Every nonempty structural producer in the same
`ShellPhysicalBinding` ledger is mandatory. Point masses and rigid bodies belong
to the physical owner and do not acquire phantom constitutive participants.

The publisher retains the immutable physical and actual rigid bindings, a pointer
to the sole `FENodalState`, and borrowed participant pointers. The composing case
must keep that owner and all participants alive through publisher destruction.
The solid batch's already-prepared immutable model remains its sole material
authority; its complete contribution snapshot must match the common ledger.
The source adapter remains responsible for original material admission.

Startup authenticates configuration, qualification, complete family scopes,
actual source buffers, current epoch-zero M/J, rigid roots and the complete CIN
witness roster. All fallible checks and temporary readbacks finish before the
first private participant claim. The mapped shell, TYPE25 and TYPE13 initial accepted assemblies
bind their live sources; the composing case discards that proof attempt before
attaching the common publisher. No accepted interval is advanced by that proof.

For each real attempt all accepted contributors assemble on the existing owner
token, and each evaluates its candidate from the same prepared endpoint. The
publisher stages their exact signed diagnostics. It repeats complete preflight
and validates the capture receipt before the one owner commit. Only infallible
existing participant selector swaps follow that commit. Numerical rejection,
missing last contributor or rejected capture discards all trials; accepted
history, activity, force caches and nodal state stay unchanged. CUDA failure
poisons the affected publication; device recovery is not promised.

Physical diagnostics explicitly have unavailable kinetic energy. The old
fixed-coefficient shell reduction cannot represent current CIN transfers and
rigid-primary coefficients. A later observer must qualify those current fields.
The old shell diagnostics and transaction entry points remain separate and
reject this profile. Accepted shell activity uses the same attached objects and
complete physical inventory, including coincident layers and rigid skins.

The publisher allocates no CUDA storage. Its forecast charges the additional
host state and conservative shared-control reservation plus the largest initial
proof phase. Existing participant arenas, immutable source backing and owner
storage are shared and remain in the composing case's overall memory forecast.
No allocation, material arithmetic, new clock, timestep selection or load-path
closure is introduced by this coordinator.

Qualification uses one tiny actual owner with complete QEPH/T3/QBAT, TYPE25,
TYPE13 and all three solid families. It checks late attach failure without any
claim, capture rejection, last-contributor rejection, exact accepted histories
and activity across discard, then retry and a single successful publication.
Native mechanics comparisons stay in the owning participant gates; this gate
tests their combined transaction. Author checks are host/syntax only; root owns
the actual CUDA and affected legacy qualification.
